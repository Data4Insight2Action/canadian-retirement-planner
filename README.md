# Canadian Retirement Income & Tax Planner

A free educational tool for exploring Canadian retirement income, taxes, government benefits, and investment withdrawals.

## Try the App

👉 https://ericwu889.github.io/canadian-retirement-planner/

No registration.  
No account required.  
No personal financial information is uploaded or transmitted.  
Calculations run locally in your browser.

## What It Covers

The planner can help you explore retirement scenarios involving:

- CPP and QPP
- OAS
- Employer pensions
- RRIF withdrawals
- TFSA withdrawals
- Non-registered investments
- Federal and provincial income taxes
- Pension income splitting
- Investment growth and depletion over time

## Purpose

This app is intended for educational and scenario-planning purposes only.

It is not financial, investment, legal, or tax advice.
